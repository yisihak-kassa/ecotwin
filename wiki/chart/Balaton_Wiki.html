Flow

Spatial wind forcing + bathymetry and geometry → WAVE_1 wave model → reed response

Lake geometry supplies local depth and shoreline-derived directional fetch to the wave model. It also supplies the depth, elevation and reed-edge geometry needed by the reed-response model.
